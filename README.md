# Zombies With Flint & Steel (Forge 1.20.1)

Zombies armed with Flint & Steel bring chaos and fire to your world, igniting blocks, players, villagers, and primed explosives in battle!

---

## 🌟 Features

* **Pyromaniac Undead**: Zombies spawn holding a Flint & Steel and actively set fire to blocks in front of fleeing targets.
* **Combustion Combat**: Directly ignites players, villagers, and iron golems upon reaching close quarters.
* **TNT Detonation**: Will deliberately ignite nearby placed TNT to blast through player barricades.
* **Durability & Item Wear**: Flint & Steel loses durability on each strike and breaks when depleted.
